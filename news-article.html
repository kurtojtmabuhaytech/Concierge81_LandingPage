‹ Newsroom Product release · September 22, 2026

Concierge81 launches Voice Mode for lobby kiosks.

Guests can now simply ask out loud. Voice Mode listens, answers in natural speech, and shows the reply on screen — in English, Japanese, and Filipino.

Today we're releasing Voice Mode for concierge81 kiosks. Until now, guests had to type their question on a touchscreen. With Voice Mode, they can walk up and ask — "What time is breakfast?" — and get a spoken answer in seconds.

Voice Mode is available on all plans starting today, in every region, with no change to pricing. Existing topics and hand-off rules carry over automatically.

What changes for front-desk teams

Busy lobbies were the most common reason teams hesitated to deploy a kiosk: guests carrying bags don't want to stop and type. Voice Mode removes that step, while every answer still appears on screen for guests who prefer to read.

Availability

  • Available today on all plans — no extra cost.
  • English, Japanese, and Filipino at launch; Korean coming next.
  • Works on existing concierge81 kiosks after a software update.
